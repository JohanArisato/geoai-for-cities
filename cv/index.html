Research

Who Gets the Shade? Ranking New York Neighborhoods for Tree Planting2026
Working paper. Code and data
  • Analyzed heat exposure, tree canopy, poverty and heat-related emergency visits across New York City's 42 UHF neighborhoods using public data from the NYC Environment & Health Data Portal.
  • Heat-related emergency visits were more strongly correlated with poverty (r = 0.59) than with surface temperature (r = 0.20). Tree canopy was associated with lower surface temperature (−0.17 °F per percentage point).
  • Compared five prioritization rules and 5,000 random weightings. The number of high-poverty neighborhoods among the first ten selected ranged from four to nine depending on the rule.
  • Methods: Moran's I and LISA, spatial lag and error models, leave-one-borough-out cross-validation.
